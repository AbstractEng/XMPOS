# XMP OS tutorial scripts

## 1. Meet your new workflow

Find Clips, Play, Steps and Sound using the hardware controls.

1. **Start with the music**

   On screen: MAIN opens Clips: tracks across, scenes down.

   Narration: Let's find our way around. Main brings you to Clips: tracks across, scenes down. This is where your parts come together.

2. **Use MENU and a pad**

   On screen: MENU + PAD 13 → Play. The top row opens track pages.

   Narration: Hold Menu and press pad thirteen for Play. The top row takes you to the selected track's pages.

3. **Pick a track, play a sound**

   On screen: A selects track 1. PAD 1 plays its kick.

   Narration: A selects track one. Try pad one. Those same sixteen pads will play sounds, enter steps, or launch clips, depending on the page.

4. **Go straight to Steps**

   On screen: 16 LEVEL opens Steps. MAIN returns to Clips.

   Narration: Sixteen Level takes you straight to Steps. Main gets you back to Clips. Your notes stay with the project.

5. **Shape the sound**

   On screen: MENU + PAD 16 → Sound. PAD 13 chooses Kit; Q-LINK changes the knob page.

   Narration: Hold Menu and press pad sixteen for Sound. Choose Kit with pad thirteen, then press Q-Link and read the four knob labels.

6. **Ready for a beat**

   On screen: MAIN → Clips. Next: build a loop with the pads.

   Narration: That's enough navigation to start making something. Back to Main, and let's put a beat on those pads.

## 2. A beat without poking the screen

Create a 16-step kick and snare loop with the pads.

1. **Create the first clip**

   On screen: A → 16 LEVEL → press DATA DIAL to create a clip.

   Narration: Start on a fresh practice project. Press A, Sixteen Level, then the data dial to create your first clip.

2. **Choose the kick**

   On screen: Hold SHIFT + PAD 1. Release SHIFT before entering steps.

   Narration: Hold Shift and hit pad one to select the kick. Release Shift. Now the pads enter steps instead of changing sounds.

3. **Four on the floor**

   On screen: PAD 13, 9, 5, 1 = steps 1, 5, 9, 13.

   Narration: Time runs from the top row down. Pads thirteen, nine, five, and one give us four steady kicks.

4. **Add the backbeat**

   On screen: SHIFT + PAD 2 chooses Snare. PAD 9 and 1 enter its hits.

   Narration: Shift and pad two selects the snare. Release Shift, then hit pads nine and one. There's your backbeat.

5. **Hear the loop**

   On screen: PLAY START plays from the beginning.

   Narration: Press Play Start. Simple, but it already gives us something to build on.

6. **Keep your work**

   On screen: STOP. Wait for Saved before leaving the session.

   Narration: Press Stop and wait for Saved. On the Live, look for Saved on stick before restarting or switching off.

## 3. Make the hats breathe

Add eighth-note hats and vary a hit's velocity.

1. **Choose the hat**

   On screen: In Steps, hold SHIFT + PAD 3 to select the closed hat.

   Narration: Back in Steps, hold Shift and pad three for the closed hat. We're adding to the beat, keeping the kick and snare.

2. **Fill the first half**

   On screen: PAD 13, 15, 9, 11 = steps 1, 3, 5, 7.

   Narration: Try eighth notes. Pads thirteen, fifteen, nine, and eleven fill the first half of the bar.

3. **Finish the bar**

   On screen: PAD 5, 7, 1, 3 = steps 9, 11, 13, 15.

   Narration: Then pads five, seven, one, and three finish it. The last hit you entered is selected.

4. **Soften the last hat**

   On screen: Read the Q-Link labels. On the Step page, turn Q1 down.

   Narration: With the Step knob page showing, turn Q one down. That last hat gets softer, which gives the loop a little movement.

5. **Listen to the difference**

   On screen: PLAY START. Hear the softer hat before the loop returns.

   Narration: Play Start. Listen for the softer hit just before the bar comes round again.

6. **Compare safely**

   On screen: STOP → UNDO → SHIFT + UNDO to redo.

   Narration: Stop, Undo, then Shift and Undo to bring it back. Keep the version that feels better to you.

## 4. Give the drums a bass line

Choose a synth for a new track and enter pitched notes with the pads.

1. **Choose a new track**

   On screen: B selects track 2. MENU + PAD 16 opens its machine choices.

   Narration: Let's give those drums some company. B selects track two. Hold Menu and press pad sixteen for Sound, with the machine choices for this new track.

2. **Make it a synth**

   On screen: PAD 15 chooses Synth. SHIFT + PAD 1 auditions a note.

   Narration: Choose Synth with pad fifteen. Hold Shift and play pad one to hear the current sound.

3. **Create its pattern**

   On screen: 16 LEVEL → press DATA DIAL. SHIFT + PAD 1 selects C3.

   Narration: Sixteen Level, then press the dial to create a clip. Shift and pad one selects C three for editing.

4. **Give it some space**

   On screen: PAD 13 and 5 enter steps 1 and 9.

   Narration: Put notes on pads thirteen and five. That's steps one and nine. Leave some space for the drums.

5. **Try another pitch**

   On screen: SHIFT + PAD 3 selects E3; PAD 7 enters step 11.

   Narration: Shift and pad three selects E three. Release Shift and hit pad seven for a little answer on step eleven.

6. **Hear the scene**

   On screen: MAIN → DATA DIAL launches the selected scene. STOP finishes.

   Narration: Main, then press the dial to hear the scene together. Stop when you're done. A few notes can go a long way.

## 5. Make a variation, keep the original

Copy a drum clip and change the copy.

1. **Focus the original**

   On screen: MAIN → A. Keep scene 1 selected.

   Narration: Let's make a second version without losing the first. Main, then A puts the focus back on the drums.

2. **Copy to the next scene**

   On screen: COPY → PAD 9 copies into track 1, scene 2.

   Narration: Press Copy, then pad nine. Your drum clip now has an independent copy in the next scene.

3. **Open the copy**

   On screen: 16 LEVEL. SHIFT + PAD 2 selects the snare.

   Narration: Open Steps with Sixteen Level, and choose the snare with Shift and pad two.

4. **Add a little push**

   On screen: PAD 3 adds a snare on step 15.

   Narration: Pad three adds a snare just before the bar turns round. A small change is enough to make a different section.

5. **Try it both ways**

   On screen: UNDO removes the new hit. SHIFT + UNDO restores it.

   Narration: Undo takes that hit out. Shift and Undo brings it back. The original clip hasn't changed.

6. **Back to the clips**

   On screen: MAIN shows both versions in the same track.

   Narration: Press Main. Both versions are there, ready to launch. Next we'll play them as parts of a track.

## 6. Play the clip grid

Launch parts and scenes with the 4×4 pads.

1. **Read the pad window**

   On screen: Top row: scene 1. PAD 13 launches track 1's first clip.

   Narration: In Clips, the pads are a four by four window. The top row is scene one. Pad thirteen launches its drum clip.

2. **Bring in the bass**

   On screen: PAD 14 launches track 2 in the same scene.

   Narration: Pad fourteen brings in the bass. Each column is a track, so you can build things up one part at a time.

3. **Switch the drums**

   On screen: PAD 9 launches the drum variation in scene 2.

   Narration: Pad nine switches the drums to the next scene. Watch the queued state: the change lands on the launch grid.

4. **Take a part out**

   On screen: Press PAD 9 again to stop that playing clip.

   Narration: Press the playing clip's pad again to stop that track. You can leave the other parts running.

5. **Launch a whole scene**

   On screen: Press DATA DIAL to launch the selected scene.

   Narration: Press the data dial to launch the whole selected scene. Empty slots stop their tracks, so a scene can be a breakdown too.

6. **Reach the other scenes**

   On screen: + / − moves four scenes. STOP stops everything.

   Narration: Plus and minus move the pad window four scenes at a time. Press Stop to finish the jam.

## 7. Turn the parts into a song

Use pads to build a scene chain.

1. **Open Song**

   On screen: MENU + PAD 10 opens Song.

   Narration: You've got parts. Let's give them an order. Hold Menu and press pad ten for Song.

2. **Add the first section**

   On screen: PAD 13 adds scene 1 after the selected block.

   Narration: Pad thirteen adds scene one. In Song, the pads represent scenes instead of individual clips.

3. **Add a change**

   On screen: PAD 14 adds scene 2, then PAD 13 returns to scene 1.

   Narration: Pad fourteen adds scene two, and pad thirteen brings us back to scene one. A simple beginning, change, and return.

4. **Let a section run**

   On screen: Q1 adjusts repeats for the selected block.

   Narration: Turn Q one to give the selected block another repeat. You can let a good part sit for a little longer.

5. **Play the chain**

   On screen: PLAY START plays the song from the beginning.

   Narration: Press Play Start to hear the chain from the beginning. The blocks tell you which scene comes next.

6. **Keep it editable**

   On screen: STOP. UNDO reverses the last song edit.

   Narration: Stop when you've heard enough. The song stays editable, and Undo can take back the last arrangement change.

## 8. Make room for each part

Select, balance, mute and solo tracks using hardware.

1. **Open the mixer**

   On screen: MENU + PAD 11 → Mix. PAD 14 selects track 2.

   Narration: Hold Menu and press pad eleven for Mix. The top two pad rows select tracks. Pad fourteen selects the bass on track two.

2. **Set the balance**

   On screen: Q1 controls the selected track's level.

   Narration: Turn Q one down a little. We're making room for the drums, rather than making everything louder.

3. **Hear the mix**

   On screen: PLAY START. Keep an eye on the selected strip.

   Narration: Play Start. Listen to the drums and bass together while you watch their strips.

4. **Mute the drums**

   On screen: PAD 5 mutes track 1; press again to bring it back.

   Narration: Pad five mutes the drums. Press it again to bring them back. The bottom two rows are your mute controls.

5. **Hear one part alone**

   On screen: SHIFT + PAD 5 solos track 1. Repeat to leave solo.

   Narration: Hold Shift and pad five to solo the drums. Do it again to hear the full mix.

6. **Save the balance**

   On screen: STOP, then wait for Saved.

   Narration: Press Stop and let the save finish. That's a useful mix check without leaving the hardware controls.

## 9. Play a part into the track

Record and overdub notes from the pads.

1. **Give the part a track**

   On screen: C → MENU + PAD 16 → PAD 15 chooses Synth on track 3.

   Narration: Let's play a part instead of entering steps. C selects track three. Hold Menu and press pad sixteen, then choose Synth with pad fifteen.

2. **Find a phrase**

   On screen: SHIFT + MAIN opens Play. Try PAD 1, 3 and 5.

   Narration: Shift and Main opens Play. Try pads one, three, and five. Keep it short enough to remember.

3. **Start a take**

   On screen: REC starts recording. Watch the count-in before playing.

   Narration: Press Record. Watch the count-in, and let the first bar settle before you play.

4. **Play the phrase**

   On screen: Play PAD 1, 3 and 5, then REC to finish the take.

   Narration: Play that little phrase on one, three, and five. Press Record again to finish the take and turn it into a clip.

5. **Add one more note**

   On screen: OVER DUB adds to the playing clip. PAD 7 adds a note.

   Narration: Press Overdub and add pad seven. Press Overdub again when you've added enough.

6. **Check the result**

   On screen: STOP → 16 LEVEL shows the recorded notes. UNDO is available.

   Narration: Stop, then Sixteen Level shows what you played. If a take doesn't work, Undo gives you another go.

## 10. Change the kit, keep the beat

Load a whole kit from Browse without rewriting the pattern.

1. **Go to the drum sound**

   On screen: A → MENU + PAD 16 opens the drum track's Sound page.

   Narration: Want a different drum character? Press A, then hold Menu and press pad sixteen. We're changing the sounds, keeping the beat.

2. **Open Browse**

   On screen: SHIFT + MENU opens Browse.

   Narration: Shift and Menu opens Browse. The library is where your samples, kits, and saved projects live.

3. **Show the kits**

   On screen: In Browse, SHIFT + PAD 11 selects Kits.

   Narration: Hold Shift and pad eleven to show Kits. The pad labels on screen tell you what each library shortcut does.

4. **Choose and load**

   On screen: PAD 14 selects the second listed kit. Press DATA DIAL to load.

   Narration: Pad fourteen selects the second kit in this library. Press the data dial to load it onto the selected track.

5. **Hear the same notes**

   On screen: MAIN → PAD 13 plays the first drum clip with the new kit.

   Narration: Back to Main, then pad thirteen. Same pattern, different kit. That can change the whole feel of a part.

6. **Compare the change**

   On screen: STOP → UNDO compares the old kit; SHIFT + UNDO restores the new one.

   Narration: Stop, Undo to compare, and Shift Undo to bring the new kit back. The other tracks keep their sounds.

## 11. Shape one drum sound

Open a pad's sample and adjust its trim and tuning.

1. **Choose the sound**

   On screen: A → MENU + PAD 16 → PAD 5 selects the clap.

   Narration: Let's make one sound fit the track. On track one, open Sound and play pad five to select its clap.

2. **Open the waveform**

   On screen: Press DATA DIAL to edit the selected pad's sample.

   Narration: Press the data dial to open that pad's sample. Now you can see the waveform you're working on.

3. **Trim the beginning**

   On screen: Q1 adjusts Start. Keep the attack you want to hear.

   Narration: Turn Q one to adjust the start. Take off a little space, but keep the attack that gives the sound its punch.

4. **Tune it down**

   On screen: Q3 adjusts Tune. PAD 5 auditions the edited sound.

   Narration: Q three changes the tuning. Try a little lower, then play pad five to hear the edit.

5. **Keep the edit**

   On screen: Press DATA DIAL to keep it. UNDO can restore the original.

   Narration: Press the dial to keep the edit. You're changing this track's sound, and Undo can restore it.

6. **Hear it in context**

   On screen: SHIFT + MAIN → PAD 5 auditions the saved sound.

   Narration: Shift and Main takes you to Play. Try pad five again. Small sample edits can make a kit feel like your own.

## 12. Save it, then prove it opens

Save a named project copy and reopen it from Browse.

1. **Finish the save**

   On screen: STOP, then wait for Saved. Live: Saved on stick.

   Narration: Before leaving a session, press Stop and wait for Saved. On hardware, the message is Saved on stick.

2. **Open Project**

   On screen: MENU + PAD 5 opens Project. PAD 15 saves the current project.

   Narration: Hold Menu and press pad five for Project. Pad fifteen saves the current project.

3. **Keep a named copy**

   On screen: PAD 16 opens Save as. Enter GROOVE on the on-screen keyboard.

   Narration: Pad sixteen opens Save as. Give this version a name, so you've got something easy to find in Browse.

4. **Confirm the name**

   On screen: PAD 14 confirms the name. Wait for the save result.

   Narration: Pad fourteen confirms the name. Wait for the save result before moving on.

5. **Reopen the copy**

   On screen: In Project, PAD 14 opens saved projects. PAD 13 selects the first; DATA DIAL loads it.

   Narration: In Project, pad fourteen opens saved projects. Select the copy with pad thirteen, then press the dial to load it.

6. **Your music is still there**

   On screen: MAIN shows the reopened clips. Web: Download saved project keeps a file copy.

   Narration: Main brings back the clips. In the browser, Download saved project also gives you a file copy. Now you know the project opens again.

## 13. Swap the snare for a clap

Replace one pad sample from Browse while keeping its notes.

1. **Pick the track and pad**

   On screen: A → SHIFT + MAIN → PAD 2 selects the snare.

   Narration: Let's swap the snare for a clap, without touching the pattern. Press A, Shift Main, then pad two.

2. **Open the sample library**

   On screen: SHIFT + MENU → SHIFT + PAD 10: Samples. SHIFT + PAD 5: Search NEW.

   Narration: Shift Menu opens Browse. Shift and pad ten shows Samples; Shift and pad five opens Search. Enter NEW and confirm with pad fourteen.

3. **Select the replacement**

   On screen: Hold SHIFT + PAD 13 to select the first listed sample.

   Narration: Hold Shift and pad thirteen to select the first sample in this prepared library: the tutorial clap.

4. **Listen before loading**

   On screen: SHIFT + PLAY auditions the selected sample.

   Narration: Shift and Play auditions it. You can check the sound before assigning it to the pad.

5. **Load it onto the pad**

   On screen: PAD 2 confirms the snare as the load target. DATA DIAL loads the clap.

   Narration: Pad two selects the snare as our load target. Press the dial to load the clap. Its notes stay where they were.

6. **Hear the backbeat**

   On screen: MAIN → PAD 13 plays the clip. STOP finishes.

   Narration: Back to Main and pad thirteen. Hear how the new backbeat changes the groove. Stop when you're done; Undo can restore the snare.

## 14. Find a synth sound that fits

Choose synth presets from the physical pad grid.

1. **Open the synth**

   On screen: B → MENU + PAD 16 opens track 2's synth Sound page.

   Narration: Let's try a different colour for the bass. Press B, then hold Menu and press pad sixteen for Sound.

2. **Pick a preset**

   On screen: PAD 14 selects the second preset on the current page.

   Narration: The whole pad grid selects presets here. Pad fourteen chooses the second one on the current page.

3. **Play without changing it**

   On screen: Hold SHIFT + PAD 1, 3 and 5 to audition notes.

   Narration: Hold Shift while playing pads one, three, and five. That lets you hear the sound without choosing another preset.

4. **Try the next bank**

   On screen: + / − changes the preset bank; DATA DIAL selects a highlighted preset.

   Narration: Plus and minus move between preset banks. You can also turn the data dial to browse and press it to choose.

5. **Compare with the pattern**

   On screen: MAIN → DATA DIAL launches the scene with the new sound.

   Narration: Main and the data dial let you hear the sound with the rest of the scene. Listen for whether it fits the part.

6. **Keep the useful version**

   On screen: STOP. Preset changes are undoable.

   Narration: Press Stop. A preset replaces that sound's current settings, so use Undo if you prefer what you had before.

## 15. Choose what a track becomes

Choose Kit, Sampler, Synth or MIDI with the pads.

1. **Start with a new track**

   On screen: D → MENU + PAD 16 opens track 4's machine choices.

   Narration: A track can become a different kind of instrument. Press D for a new track, then hold Menu and press pad sixteen.

2. **Read the top row**

   On screen: PAD 13 = Kit · 14 = Sampler · 15 = Synth · 16 = MIDI.

   Narration: The top row chooses the machine: Kit, Sampler, Synth, or MIDI. Choose Sampler with pad fourteen.

3. **Play the sample as notes**

   On screen: SHIFT + MAIN → PAD 1, 3, 5 plays the sample across the scale.

   Narration: Shift Main opens Play. Pads one, three, and five play the same sample at different pitches. Replace it with your own sample when you're ready.

4. **Change an existing machine**

   On screen: Sound → hold SHIFT + MENU, then PAD 15 opens Choose machine.

   Narration: To change an existing track, return to Sound. Hold Shift and Menu, then pad fifteen opens Choose machine.

5. **See the MIDI controls**

   On screen: PAD 16 chooses MIDI. Set its channel and destination in Sound.

   Narration: Pad sixteen chooses MIDI. Sound now shows its channel and destination. A MIDI track needs a destination before its notes can play an instrument.

6. **Choose the tool for the part**

   On screen: SHIFT + MENU + PAD 15 → PAD 13 chooses Kit.

   Narration: For our next lesson, use Choose machine again and pick Kit with pad thirteen. We'll turn a loop into sounds on the pads.

## 16. Turn a loop into a pad kit

Split an original loop across four drum pads.

1. **Prepare the loop**

   On screen: D → Sound → PAD 1. Browse → Samples → Search LOOP; clear any old text.

   Narration: Press D for our new Kit track, then choose pad one on Sound. Open Browse, Samples, and Search. Clear any old text, enter LOOP, and confirm.

2. **Load the original loop**

   On screen: SHIFT + PAD 13 selects the first sample. DATA DIAL loads it.

   Narration: Shift and pad thirteen selects the prepared loop. Press the dial to load the whole loop onto pad one.

3. **Open the sample**

   On screen: MENU + PAD 16 → press DATA DIAL to open its waveform.

   Narration: Hold Menu and press pad sixteen to return to Sound, then press the dial to open the waveform.

4. **Make four slices**

   On screen: Press + three times: one sound becomes four evenly spaced slices.

   Narration: Press Plus three times for four slices. On a Kit track, this divides the edited sample evenly across successive pads.

5. **Keep them on the pads**

   On screen: Press DATA DIAL to keep the slices. SHIFT + MAIN opens Play.

   Narration: Press the dial to keep those slices, then Shift Main opens Play. The loop is ready to rearrange with your fingers.

6. **Make something new**

   On screen: Play PAD 1, 3, 2, 4. STOP, then wait for Saved.

   Narration: Try one, three, two, four. You've got pieces of the original loop, in a different order. Stop and wait for Saved. Now make a beat with them.
