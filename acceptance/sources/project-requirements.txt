XMP OS community requirements

These statements describe the versioned product requirements. They contain no
conversation excerpts or private source records. Stable S identifiers support
acceptance traceability.

S1 Extensibility: support original effects, instruments, interfaces and workflows.
S2 Platform: build an original music workstation and OS framework; Live Mk1 is the first qualified hardware target.
S3 Distribution: publish only distributable original and properly licensed components; keep owner-supplied vendor files local.
S4 Community: make the framework understandable, customizable and redistributable under its component licences.
S5 Music workflow: support sampling, sequencing, scenes, arrangement, performance, persistence and export.
S6 Device boundaries: develop without instruments by default; exclude Force and unqualified models from device operations.
S7 Identity: the project is XMP OS, an extensible music platform.
S8 Specification: maintain versioned documentation, compatibility records and executable acceptance tests.
