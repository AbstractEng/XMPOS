Workspaces

Sessions are anonymous visits, not unique people. A reload starts a new session; inactivity for 30 minutes ends it. Active time includes interaction within the last minute or playback/recording, including background playback. Idle tabs do not add active time. Foreground time includes visible idle time. Periods group sessions by their start date. Browser privacy preferences, opt-outs, blockers and closed tabs can reduce reported totals. Reports are cumulative snapshots, so retries do not count the same activity twice. Data is retained for 90 days and cleaned daily.
